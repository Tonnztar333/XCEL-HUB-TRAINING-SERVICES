About

About Xcel Hub Training Services

Xcel Hub Training Services is a professional training and tutorial center based in Cubao, Quezon City. Since 2018, it has been committed to helping beginners, freelancers, jobseekers, professionals, and organizations build practical digital literacy and job-ready computer skills. The training center provides hands-on learning in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, Data Management, Data Analysis, and other essential computer skills.

Location: Unit 2F Saint Martin Building, West Point Street, Cubao, Quezon City, Metro Manila 1102.

Xcel Hub offers both online and face-to-face learning options, making training more flexible and accessible for different learners.

Vision

To become a trusted and sought-after training partner in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, and data reporting by empowering individuals and organizations to become more productive, efficient, and data-driven.

Mission

To help individuals and organizations work smarter with data by providing custom-tailored training programs that are aligned with their goals, industry context, and real-world reporting needs. Xcel Hub focuses on building confidence, efficiency, and long-term digital capability among its learners.
