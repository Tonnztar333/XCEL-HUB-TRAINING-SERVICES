About Xcel Hub

Discover Xcel Hub Training Services

Located in Cubao, Quezon City, Xcel Hub is a premier training center dedicated to fostering digital literacy and developing workforce-ready technology competencies. Since our founding in 2018, we've supported thousands of learners—from entry-level professionals to career-switchers—through comprehensive training in Excel, Google Sheets, data analytics, and workplace productivity tools.

Our commitment to personalized education sets us apart. With intimate class sizes capped at six participants, each learner receives individualized attention and customized guidance. We offer multiple learning pathways including flexible scheduling, hybrid delivery options, and industry-recognized certifications through our Certiport Testing Center partnership—empowering you to thrive in an increasingly digital-driven professional environment.
